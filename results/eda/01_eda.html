def plot_trip(ax, part_dir, tid):
    t = load_trip(part_dir / tid)
    tr, nodes, arcs, r = t["track"], t["nodes"], t["arcs"], t["route"].arc_id.values
    proj = make_projector(tr.lon.mean(), tr.lat.mean())
    nx, ny = proj(nodes.lon.values, nodes.lat.values)
    px, py = proj(tr.lon.values, tr.lat.values)
    a, b = arcs.from_node.values[r], arcs.to_node.values[r]
    for i in range(len(r)):
        ax.plot([nx[a[i]], nx[b[i]]], [ny[a[i]], ny[b[i]]], color="green", lw=2)
    ax.scatter(px, py, s=3, color="blue")
    ax.set_title(f"trip {tid}"); ax.set_aspect("equal")

9. Findings and decisions¶

All figures are from results/eda/trip_stats.csv and pooled_quantiles.csv (deep run on 80 train + 10 student_test trips). Evaluator trips were never read.

Findings¶

Area What we found Evidence
Integrity No NaN or invalid coordinates, timestamps strictly increasing, no invalid arc/node references, no isolated nodes, all 4 files present for all 90 trips sections 1-2, 4
Totals train 208,417 GPS / 31,336 route arcs; student_test 19,160 / 3,477 section 1
Timing Median step 1 s, but 7,286 train and 264 student intervals exceed 1 s. Gaps are extreme: trip 90 has 42 h, trip 78 has 14.7 h (separate drives stored as one trip). 23 of 90 trips have a gap over 60 s, yet the median trip spends 0% of its time in such gaps section 2
Speed Some trips have steps up to 180 m/s, which are GPS jumps. Motorway driving (45-55 m/s) is real, so a fixed speed cutoff is wrong section 2
Geography Trips span 1.4 to 78.5 km across several continents section 3
Network Per-trip networks, median ~20 weakly connected components, 1,775 duplicate arcs and 22 self-loops in train. Largest strongly connected part holds 96% of nodes; 95% of arcs are two-way section 4
Route 10 train + 1 student trips have a few unconnected consecutive route arcs (worst 98.1% connected). Routes revisit arcs (train 67, student 255 repeated entries). Route has ~5x fewer arcs than GPS points. All route arcs lie in the main strongly connected component and none are dead ends section 5
GPS vs route Typical trip: 99% of points within 30 m of the route. Outliers: trip 54 (11% within 30 m, GPS far from every road) and trip 33 (29%, GPS near roads but not near its route, so the route may cover only part of the recording; unconfirmed) sections 6-7
Candidate radius Per-trip median nearest-arc distance: p95 12.9 m, p99 18.2 m. Pooled train tail is heavy (p99 97 m) because of a few trips (002, 054, 081, 096, 043, 084); 73 of 90 trips have every point within 50 m of some arc pooled_quantiles.csv

Decisions¶

  1. Labels: build per-point labels with a monotone, order-aware alignment of GPS points to route positions (allowing revisits), never by row index or plain nearest arc.
  2. Gaps: split each trip into segments at gaps over 60 s; no features, HMM transitions or interpolation cross a segment boundary.
  3. Projection: use a separate local metric projection per trip; never use degrees as distance.
  4. Candidates: model one row per (trip, observation, arc) with a binary target, because arc IDs are trip-local. Start with a 50 m search radius plus a k-nearest fallback, and report candidate miss rate at 30, 50 and 100 m.
  5. Network cleaning: collapse duplicate arcs and drop self-loops for candidate search, keeping a map to the original arc IDs, since the reference route uses those IDs.
  6. HMM: restrict paths to the main strongly connected component and add a straight-line-distance fallback transition when no path exists.
  7. Outlier detection: flag a GPS spike only if the speed is implausible in and out of the point and the direction reverses (a fixed speed cutoff is not used).
  8. Validation: split by trip (GroupKFold on trip_id); report per-trip-averaged metrics as well as pooled ones, because a few long trips dominate pooled counts.
  9. Suspect trips: inspect 33 and 54 on the maps first; report results with and without them, and exclude them from training only if the maps confirm label errors.

Limits to state honestly¶

  • The reference route is treated as reference, not independently verified truth (11 trips have route breaks).
  • Student_test is cleaner than train (no long gaps or implausible speeds), so its scores will likely be optimistic.
  • No model results exist yet; this notebook covers data understanding only.
